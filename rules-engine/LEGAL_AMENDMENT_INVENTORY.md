## Schedule verification

The principal First Schedule contains Table I weight/volume MPE bands and Table II length/area/number MPE rules. The engine now executes Table I and explicitly returns `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` for Table II measurement cases until the separate length/area/number evaluator is implemented.

The principal Second Schedule entries currently encoded include the published commodity categories from the accessible Rules text: baby food, weaning food, biscuits, bread/brown bread, butter and margarine, cereals and pulses, coffee, tea, beverage-forming materials, edible oils/vanaspati/ghee, milk powder, non-soapy detergents, rice/flour/atta/rawa/suji, salt, soaps, aerated soft drinks/non-alcoholic beverages, mineral/drinking water, cement, and paint categories.

Unknown commodities are never assumed to have unrestricted packaging sizes. If a future amendment changes a schedule entry, the entry must receive a dated version rather than silently replacing the historical rule.

## Legal safety rule

A source being present in the Government archive does not by itself mean every clause has been transcribed into executable logic. Untranscribed or conflicting provisions remain `REQUIRES_LEGAL_REVIEW` / `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` rather than being guessed.
