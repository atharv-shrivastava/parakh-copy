# Second Schedule Legal Status

## Critical historical rule

The Second Schedule of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 was omitted by G.S.R. 226(E). The amended framework took effect from 1 October 2022.

Therefore the engine must NOT treat the Second Schedule as a current mandatory standard-size table for inspections dated on or after 1 October 2022.

For inspections dated on or before 30 September 2022, the historical Second Schedule remains relevant and may be evaluated against the applicable rule version.

## Implementation requirement

Second Schedule evaluation must be date-aware. A current inspection must never receive a Rule 5 / Second Schedule violation merely because its quantity is absent from the old schedule.

The existing commodity predicates remain retained as historical data, but they must be routed through the date applicability gate before producing a finding.

## Source

Official Ministry of Consumer Affairs / Gazette material: G.S.R. 226(E), 28 March 2022. The notification states that the Second Schedule shall be omitted and identifies the amended provisions' commencement as 1 October 2022.

Official consolidated Packaged Commodities Rules source: Department of Consumer Affairs, Book on Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 with all amendments.
