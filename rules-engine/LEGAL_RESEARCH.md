# Legal Research Record

## Scope

This standalone engine implements and versions the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. It is independent of OCR, frontend, backend, Replit, Grok, Gemini, or any other AI implementation.

## Authoritative source hierarchy

1. Individual Gazette notification for exact amendment wording and commencement.
2. Department of Consumer Affairs official consolidated publication for the Rules and schedules.
3. Department of Consumer Affairs archive for amendment discovery and cross-checking.

## Verified current sources

- G.S.R. 202(E), 7 March 2011, effective 1 April 2011: principal rules.
- G.S.R. 784(E), 24 October 2011, effective 1 July 2012: material changes to Rules 5, 6, 12, 19, 26 and Fourth Schedule.
- G.S.R. 881(E), 2 December 2025, effective 1 February 2026: pan masala exception under Rule 26(a).
- G.S.R. 128(E), 13 February 2026, effective 1 July 2026: Rule 6(10A) country-of-origin filter for e-commerce.
- Second Amendment Rules, 27 April 2026, effective 1 July 2027: substitution of Rule 6(10A).
- Third Amendment Rules, 29 May 2026: Rule 4 Explanation-2 and Rule 27 changes.

## Coverage policy

The engine must never infer an unstated legal exemption, threshold, commodity standard, measurement tolerance, penalty, or procedural requirement. Any provision that is identified but not fully verified is represented as requiring legal review or unable to verify.

## Important research status

The Department of Consumer Affairs archive lists additional amendments and advisories from 2011 through 2025, including amendments in 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2021 and multiple 2023 amendments. These must remain in the amendment inventory even where their exact executable effect has not yet been transcribed into the rule catalog.

The official consolidated publication is the principal schedule source. The First Schedule and Second Schedule are being transcribed into structured data incrementally. Unknown commodity categories must not be treated as unrestricted.
